METHODOLOGY NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Hyperparameters and thresholds are selected using inner temporal folds only.
The same inner folds are used for both selections; inner scores are selection scores, not unbiased generalization estimates.
All preprocessing is fitted within each training fold.
The outer fold evaluates the complete selection procedure on unseen later observations.
The 2024 holdout remains excluded.
